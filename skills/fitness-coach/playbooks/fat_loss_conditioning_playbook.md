# Fat Loss & Conditioning Playbook

This playbook outlines metabolic conditioning and fat loss coaching guidelines.

## Core Pillars
1. **Caloric Deficit**: Crucial for fat loss. Keep deficits moderate (300-500 kcal below maintenance) to preserve muscle mass.
2. **Strength Retention**: Maintain lifting intensity during fat loss phases to signal the body to retain lean muscle.
3. **NEAT (Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis)**: Focus on daily step count (target 8,000 to 12,000 steps).

## Conditioning Protocols
- **LISS (Low-Intensity Steady State Cardio)**:
  - 30-45 minutes at Zone 2 heart rate (60-70% max HR).
  - Easy recovery, low impact, promotes fat oxidation.
- **HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training)**:
  - Kettlebell complexes or sprints.
  - Work-to-Rest ratio: 1:2 or 1:3 (e.g., 20s work, 40s rest).
  - Limit to 1-2 sessions per week to prevent nervous system fatigue.
