# Hypertrophy Training Playbook

This playbook outlines the fundamental principles for coaching muscle growth (hypertrophy).

## Core Principles
1. **Mechanical Tension**: The primary driver of hypertrophy. Must focus on heavy lifting (65-85% 1RM) through a full range of motion.
2. **Progressive Overload**: Systematically increasing the stimulus over time. Scribes:
   - Add load (weight)
   - Add volume (sets or reps)
   - Improve execution and control
3. **Volume Guidelines**: 10-20 working sets per muscle group per week, depending on recovery capabilities.

## Progression Protocol
- **Double Progression Method**: 
  - Choose a target rep range (e.g., 8 to 12 reps).
  - Use a weight that allows at least 8 reps.
  - When the user can perform all sets at the upper limit (12 reps) with good form, increase the load by 2.5-5% and restart at the lower limit (8 reps).
- **Proximity to Failure**: Sets should end with 1 to 3 Reps in Reserve (RIR).
