# Mobility & Recovery Playbook

This playbook outlines joint mobility prep, dynamic warmups, and nervous system recovery techniques.

## Active Warmup Sequence
Before every workout, recommend a general dynamic warmup (5-10 minutes):
1. **Cat-Cow**: 10 repetitions to mobilize the spine.
2. **World's Greatest Stretch**: 5 per side to open hips and thoracic spine.
3. **Band Pull-aparts**: 15 reps to activate the upper back and rotator cuffs.
4. **Bodyweight Squats**: 10 reps to warm up the knees and hips.

## Post-Workout Recovery
- **Cooldown**: 3-5 minutes of parasympathetic box breathing (inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s, hold 4s).
- **Sleep**: 7-9 hours of quality sleep is the single most powerful recovery tool.
- **Nutrition**: Consuming 1.6-2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight, spread evenly throughout the day.
- **Hydration**: Drink 3-4 liters of water daily, scaling up based on workout intensity.
