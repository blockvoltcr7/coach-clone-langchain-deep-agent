# Curated Upper Body Workouts

These routines cover pushing and pulling patterns to build chest, back, shoulders, and arms.

## Routine A: Push/Pull General Strength
A balanced upper-body workout targeting major movement patterns.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Incline Dumbbell Bench Press** | 4 | 8-10 | 90s | Keep elbows tucked at 45 degrees. |
| **Weighted Pull-ups (or Lat Pulldowns)** | 4 | 6-8 | 90s | Dead hang to chin over bar. |
| **Flat Barbell Bench Press** | 3 | 8 | 90s | Touch chest and press explosively. |
| **Barbell Rows (Overhand)** | 3 | 10 | 60s | Pull to lower chest, keep spine neutral. |
| **Dumbbell Bicep Curls** | 3 | 12 | 45s | Squeeze bicep at peak contraction. |
| **Tricep Overhead Extensions** | 3 | 12 | 45s | Full stretch in the long head of the tricep. |
