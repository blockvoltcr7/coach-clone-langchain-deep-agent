# Curated Ab Workouts

These routines focus on core stability, anterior/posterior chain integration, and rotational power.

## Routine A: Core Stability & Anti-Rotation
Focuses on isometric strength and resisting movement to protect the lower back.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hanging Leg Raises** | 3 | 10-12 | 60s | Controlled descent, avoid swinging. |
| **Ab Wheel Rollouts** | 3 | 8-10 | 60s | Keep hips tucked, do not arch lower back. |
| **Pallof Press** | 3 | 12 (per side) | 45s | Hold contraction for 2s at extension. |
| **RKC Plank** | 3 | 20s | 45s | Max tension: pull elbows to toes, squeeze glutes. |

## Routine B: Rotational Power & Flexion
Focuses on dynamic contraction and oblique integration.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kettlebell Windmill** | 3 | 6-8 (per side) | 60s | Keep eyes on the kettlebell, hinge hips back. |
| **Decline Bench Crunches** | 3 | 15 | 45s | Focus on squeezing the abs, not pulling the neck. |
| **Woodchoppers (Cable or Band)**| 3 | 12 (per side) | 45s | Explode on rotation, control the return. |
