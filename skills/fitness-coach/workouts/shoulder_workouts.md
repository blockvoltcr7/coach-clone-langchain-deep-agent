# Curated Shoulder Workouts

These routines prioritize shoulder health, deltoid hypertrophy, and overhead stability.

## Routine A: Overhead Power & Lateral Hypertrophy
Focuses on overhead strength and broad lateral deltoid growth.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standing Military Press** | 4 | 6-8 | 90s | Clean reps, brace core, lock out at the top. |
| **Dumbbell Lateral Raises** | 4 | 12-15 | 60s | Lead with elbows, control the eccentric phase. |
| **Rear Delt Cable Flyes** | 3 | 15-20 | 45s | High reps to target the posterior deltoid. |
| **Dumbbell Cuban Press** | 3 | 12 | 60s | Promotes external rotator cuff health. |

## Routine B: Overhead Stability & Structural Health
Focuses on isometric shoulder stability and joint longevity.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kettlebell Kettlebell Halo** | 3 | 8 (each direction) | 45s | Keep kettlebell close to your head. |
| **Single-Arm KB Press (Bottom-Up)**| 3 | 8 (per side) | 60s | Tests and improves shoulder stability and grip. |
| **Face Pulls with Band/Cable** | 4 | 15 | 45s | Squeeze shoulder blades together at the back. |
