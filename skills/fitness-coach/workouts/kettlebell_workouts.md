# Curated Kettlebell Workouts

These routines highlight explosive power, metabolic conditioning, and unilateral stability using kettlebells.

## Routine A: Kettlebell Conditioning & Power
A high-intensity circuit targeting cardiovascular and explosive power.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Kettlebell Swings (Two-Handed)** | 5 | 20 | 45s | Power from the hips; do not squat the weight. |
| **Kettlebell Clean & Press** | 4 | 8 (per side) | 60s | Transition smoothly from clean to strict press. |
| **Goblet Squats** | 4 | 12 | 60s | Keep kettlebell close to chest, sink deep. |
| **Turkish Get-ups (TGU)** | 3 | 3 (per side) | 90s | Slow and technical; focus on stability. |

## Routine B: Single-Kettlebell Complex
Perform as a flow or complex. Complete all exercises on one side before resting and switching sides.

- **Kettlebell Snatch** x 5 reps
- **Kettlebell Clean & Lunge** x 5 reps (lunge back on active leg)
- **Kettlebell Push Press** x 5 reps
- **Kettlebell Goblet Squat** x 5 reps
- *Rest 90 seconds. Do 3 to 5 rounds per side.*
