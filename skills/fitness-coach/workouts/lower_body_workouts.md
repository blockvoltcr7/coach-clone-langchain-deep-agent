# Curated Lower Body Workouts

These routines target the quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, and calves while focusing on athletic movement patterns.

## Routine A: Squat-Dominant & Quad Focus
Focuses on bilateral squats and unilateral quad-dominant patterns.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Barbell Back Squat** | 4 | 6-8 | 120s | Full depth (below parallel if possible), control down. |
| **Bulgarian Split Squats** | 3 | 10 (per side) | 90s | Keep torso slightly leaned forward for glute/quad. |
| **Leg Press (Wide/Low Stance)** | 3 | 12-15 | 60s | Push through heels, don't lock knees at the top. |
| **Standing Calf Raises** | 4 | 15 | 45s | Hold 1s stretch at bottom, 1s contraction at top. |

## Routine B: Hinge-Dominant & Posterior Chain
Focuses on posterior chain (glutes/hamstrings) development and hip extension.

| Exercise | Sets | Reps | Rest | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Romanian Deadlifts (Barbell)**| 4 | 8-10 | 90s | Hinge back, keep back flat, stretch hamstrings. |
| **Hip Thrusts (Barbell)** | 3 | 10-12 | 90s | Squeeze glutes at top, hold for 1s. |
| **Lying Leg Curls** | 3 | 12 | 60s | Controlled eccentric (3 seconds down). |
